StopLoss

Reactive stop-loss on the real Uniswap V3 WETH/USDC pool (Base Sepolia). Deposit WETH, set a stop price — a reactive contract on Lasna watches the pool's Swap events and, the moment the price drops below your stop, sells your WETH for USDC. No oracle, no off-chain bot.

Live data loads without a wallet. Connect to deposit WETH and create stop-loss orders. The app switches MetaMask to Base Sepolia (chain ID 84532).

2. Approve vault & deposit WETH

Approval is for the exact amount you enter — never unlimited. Withdrawing returns unallocated vault WETH to your wallet.

3. Create stop-loss order

Enter a stop price to see the on-chain threshold.

After you create an order, the stop must be armed on the reactive contract (Lasna) — that is an owner step done from the deployer, not from this UI. One stop can be armed at a time. See the demo flow in DEMO.md. Once armed, the reactive contract itself catches the price crossing and the callback sells your WETH for USDC.
